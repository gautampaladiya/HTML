About
Born into the prominent Bhatt family in Mumbai, she is the daughter of acclaimed filmmaker Mahesh Bhatt and actress Soni Razdan. She formally entered the film industry as a teenager but also manages an entrepreneurial career, including her sustainable children's clothing brand, Ed-a-Mamma.
